      York-based, selective professional development opportunity for women 5–10
      years out of school. In small group format, invitees engage with
      significant senior women in architecture, engineering, construction, real
      estate, law, and financial services to advance their own career goals.
      Participants share common experiences and trade personal strategies for
      success.
