A Girl is a Fellow Here: 100 Women Architects in the Studio of Frank Lloyd Wright

At a time when few architectural firms would hire women, Frank Lloyd Wright unhesitatingly employed women, giving them both training and the opportunity to practice. Ultimately, over 100 women architects and designers worked with Wright, many of them going on to remarkable careers of their own. This film focuses on six of those women—Marion Mahony, Isabel Roberts, Lois Gottlieb, Jane Duncombe, Eleanore Pettersen, and Read Weber.

Learn More

Eames: The Architect and the Painter

BWAF helped fund Eames: The Architect and the Painter. Fellow Jason Cohn (2006), researched the life and work of Ray (Kaiser) Eames, partner in the legendary design team, focusing on issues of artistic collaboration.
