  <p>Norma Sklarek had many “firsts”. She was often credited at the start of her
  career as the first Black Women architect to be licensed in the United States.
  That distinction actually goes to Beverly Greene – Norma was the 3rd. But it
  didn’t matter. Young black girls read her name in the likes of Ebony Magazine
  – a staple publication in every black household at the time – when she was
  included in their 1958 article on “Successful Young Architects”. As more and
  more discovered her career, she became their role model.</p>
