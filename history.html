History

  • 2002

    Founded — February 21st with support from Beverly Willis.

  • 2005

    Grant program begins — Projects funded from 2005 to 2013 include USA: Modern Architectures in History, A Girl Is A Fellow Here, and Charles & Ray Eames.

  • 2006

    National Building Museum and BWAF begin collaboration — The women of architecture program began at the nation’s premium institution for building excellence, in observance of Women’s History Month.
