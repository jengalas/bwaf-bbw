description: "Frank Lloyd Wright, America's most influential architect, died in 1959
  at 92, just before the completion of the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum. Preparations
  for the museum's 50th anniversary celebration uncovered the fact that 100 women
  worked with Wright during his career. Who were these women? \n\nAt a time when few
  architectural firms would hire women, Frank Lloyd Wright unhesitatingly employed
  women, giving them both training and the opportunity to practice. Ultimately, over
  100 women architects and designers worked with Wright, many of them going on to
  remarkable careers of their own. This film focuses on six of those women—Marion
  Mahony, Isabel Roberts, Lois Gottlieb, Jane Duncombe, Eleanore Pettersen, and Read
  Weber."
