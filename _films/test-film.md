title: A Girl is a Fellow Here. 100 Women Architects in the Studio of Frank Lloyd
  Wright
description: At a time when few architectural firms would hire women, Frank Lloyd
  Wright unhesitatingly employed women, giving them both training and the opportunity
  to practice. Ultimately, over 100 women architects and designers worked with Wright,
  many of them going on to remarkable careers of their own. This film focuses on six
  of those women—Marion Mahony, Isabel Roberts, Lois Gottlieb, Jane Duncombe, Eleanore
  Pettersen, and Read Weber.
